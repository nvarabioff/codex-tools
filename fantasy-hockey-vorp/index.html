2. League setup

Replacement level includes starters, utility, and bench. Allocate your bench by position (the three bench assumptions should equal bench slots). Your current no-bench setup defaults to F100, D50, G20.

3. Scoring settings

Enter the CSV header exactly as it appears in your projections file. Leave a stat out if your league does not score it. “Points per unit” may be negative.
